RULES

resource power cards are kept in the deck but are crypted when drawn, and another card drawn to replace


when an actor is defeated their card moves to the BANK on the corresponding row; if they are defeated in the home row they are crypted instead

cars are either Actor, Basic, or Tech
- basics are played in the home row
- actors are played on friendly basics with ap 0, gain AP until max and then called to action
- techs are played from the hand, sometimes with a target, and sometimes they remain on the field for a continuous effect
- actors, covert actors, cover tech can be played on friendly basics; when flipped covert tech moves to the bench

STAGES

0. Upkeep Phase; ACTOR cards gain AP + 1
1. Power Phase; Play a POWER card from CRYPT A to BANK A
2. Spell Phase; Spend POWER cards from BANKS to play ACTOR or TECH cards to BASES or the BENCH
3. Combat Phase; Actors in attack mode perform actions and advance automatically
4. Draw Phase; Discard down to 7 in HAND, draw back to 7 in HAND, discard and redraw all POWER cards
5. End Phase

COMBAT

units are deployed in either attack position or defense position

attack position units automatically spend 1 AP (this can be a floating per-turn upkeep 1 AP if they are at max AP capacity) to move forward one tempo if the square is clear, or attack whatever is there if it isn't

unit cards have a list of attacks in order of cost threshold; units have a counter indicating which move they are currently using; when the criteria for the next move is reached the counter increments;
if the current move cannot be performed the counter inctements to the next workable move, wrapping around to the start when reaching the end of the lise

attacks spend the ante AP in addition to their described cost

after being attacked enemy units will counter attack if they are able

units retreat automatically at 1 HP, when they reach the home row they convert remaining AP into HP and move to the bench to finish regenerating HP (benched units have no AP)

units that reach 0 hp are incapacitated; extra rules for bleed out if desired, otherwise incapacitaed units move to the bank row and must wait there to be rescued by a friendly unit in the same row
